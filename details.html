Exercise Details

Read through each movement before starting. Move in a controlled way, choose a comfortable pace and take a break whenever you need one.

Full-Body Workout

Bodyweight Squats: Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart. Bend your knees, lower your body, then stand up.

Wall Push-Ups: Place your hands on a wall at chest height. Bend your elbows to move closer to the wall, then push back.

Keep your back steady during each movement, and rest briefly between exercises.

Easy Cardio Workout

March in Place: Stand tall and march gently, lifting one knee at a time.

Side Steps: Step to one side, bring your other foot in, then repeat in the other direction.

Repeat the two movements at a pace that feels manageable to you.

Stretch and Relax

Overhead Stretch: Reach both arms overhead and stretch gently.

Forward Stretch: Bend forward slightly and hold a comfortable stretch.

Move gently through each stretch without bouncing, and stop if it feels uncomfortable.
